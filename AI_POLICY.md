# AI policy

We welcome the use of artificial intelligence tools, including large language
models, as aids when contributing to Z-Wave JS projects. Contributors remain
responsible for everything they submit. Maintainers remain responsible for
everything they merge and release.

Maintainers volunteer their time and expertise to review contributions. Do not
submit AI-generated content unless you have personally reviewed and understood
it.

## Autonomous agents

**Autonomous agents may not contribute to Z-Wave JS projects.** We will close
issues and pull requests that we believe were created autonomously. We may mark
automated comments as spam. This rule also applies to contributions that bypass
the provided issue or pull request templates.

## Communication on issues, pull requests, and code reviews

You may use AI tools to help you write. You must personally review anything
posted on your behalf. Keep responses to the minimum needed to explain your
intent. We may hide comments that appear to be unreviewed AI output.

You must be able to explain proposed changes in your own words. This requirement
applies to pull request descriptions and responses to questions. Review any
AI-assisted summary for technical accuracy before posting it.

Do not use AI to generate the substance of answers to maintainers. You must
understand and be able to explain your own work. You may use AI to improve the
grammar or clarity of your answer.

If context from an AI interaction is relevant, quote it with Markdown `>`
markers and identify it as AI-generated. Add your own explanation of its
relevance and implications. Do not share long excerpts.

## Non-native English speakers

AI tools can help non-native English speakers communicate. You may use them to
improve the grammar or clarity of text you wrote. Check that translations
accurately express your intent. You may include the original text in a
`<details>` block. This helps maintainers verify the translation and keeps the
discussion readable.

## Code and documentation contributions

AI tools can assist with code and documentation. Every contribution requires a
human who understands the work.

Review and understand every change before submission. You must be able to
explain every code and documentation change in your pull request. We will close
pull requests that appear to contain unreviewed AI output.

## Maintainer use of AI

Z-Wave JS projects may use AI tools for code review, issue triage, reporting,
and other project management tasks. These tools can leave comments on issues
and pull requests. Their output is fallible.

Treat an AI-generated comment like any other review comment. If it is incorrect,
say so and briefly explain why. Maintainers have the final say. Ask a maintainer
if you are unsure.

## Enforcement

We will close contributions that do not follow this policy. Repeated violations
may result in a block from contributing to Z-Wave JS projects. Contact a
maintainer if you believe we closed your contribution in error.
